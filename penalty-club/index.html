OneBridge Penalty Club

Seven students, three coaches, five penalties. Pick a side of the ball.

How to play

Striker: move to aim at the goal and tap (or press Space) to lock it. A power bar starts moving: tap again when the marker is in the gold zone. Too hard and it flies over.

Keeper: tap where you think the ball is going. Tap after the kick to react, or guess early and risk being read. Keys: arrows for low corners and the middle, Q and E for top corners.
